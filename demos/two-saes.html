  • SAE: sparse autoencoder; it rebuilds each activation from a few features, and its dictionary is the set of feature directions (the arrows).
  • Dots: 20 toy activations in 2-D. Seed: the random number that sets where training starts.
  • Reconstruction error: mean squared distance from a dot to its rebuild, 0 here because any two arrows rebuild a 2-D dot. Sparsity cost: mean summed size of the two feature values per dot; lower is sparser.

Toy illustration: a two-feature sparse dictionary fit by gradient descent in your browser on the 20 dots of the slide’s drawing. It illustrates Paulo and Belrose (2025, arXiv:2501.16615), who train SAEs on the same activations with different seeds and get different features, a result consistent with sparsity leaving several solutions rather than a single one, though not proof of it · tutorial demo · CC-BY
