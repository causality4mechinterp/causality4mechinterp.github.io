The same gender question reads differently at each rung

A probe reads gender from the activation a, zeroing a along the probe's directions moves the model's predictions, and rerunning one bio with a changed computes that bio's counterfactual about a. Each tab shows the sentence its evidence supports and what it takes to move that sentence up one rung.

All three numbers are exact for this toy; what changes from rung to rung is the sentence they support. The grey line is the one that often gets written, and Reveal shows which coordinate of a the toy's readout reads, which none of the three numbers can show. With the input given and greedy decoding, the rung-3 rerun of the swapped bio is exact; the sentence about gender also needs the swap to change gender and nothing else.
