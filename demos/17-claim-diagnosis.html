A claim’s rung is the strongest reading its verb admits. The method’s rung is what the experiment did to the model: a probe only observes it (L1), and a rerun with an activation set intervenes (L2), which with the input given and greedy decoding also computes the counterfactual about activations. A claim reaches L3 when it names the concept an activation stands for, which no rerun verifies on its own.
