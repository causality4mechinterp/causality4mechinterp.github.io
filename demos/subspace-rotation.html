  • Balls: three pictures of a ball, each placed where the encoder puts it.
  • Grey lines: the true axes, position (across) and colour (up).
  • Learned axes: the two directions a method reads the encoding along; the slider turns them.
  • Head: a linear map from the two readings to position and colour; it turns back by the same angle, or is left as is.
  • Loss: the mean squared gap between the head’s output and each ball’s true position and colour.
  • Numbers are in the toy’s units: the blue ball sits at (−13, −7), the pink at (13, 7).
