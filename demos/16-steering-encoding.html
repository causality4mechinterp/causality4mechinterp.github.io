The activation h has two true axes, the ball’s position and its colour, and each dot is one picture of the ball. An encoder whose axes are rotated by 63° fits these balls equally well, because a linear head can rotate back (demo 15). Steering the grey ball along that encoder’s learned axis 1 moves its position and its colour at once.

Try this: drag α to 11 and read the two tiles: position moved by 4.99 and colour by 9.80, the figure’s two dashed lines.
