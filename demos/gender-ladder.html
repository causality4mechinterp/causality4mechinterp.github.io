  • a: the activation at one layer for a bio; in this toy its coordinates are gender (she +1, he −1), the cue and profession content.
  • Cue: care words such as “comfort” or “families”, which travel with gender in these 200 toy bios.
  • The hidden worlds share every bio and every activation; only the readout from a to the prediction differs.
  • INLP (iterative nullspace projection): remove the probe's direction, train a new probe and remove its direction too, until no probe finds gender; then rerun.
  • Numbers under rung 3: p(nurse), the model's probability for nurse, which it predicts above 0.5; “= target” means equal to two decimals.
